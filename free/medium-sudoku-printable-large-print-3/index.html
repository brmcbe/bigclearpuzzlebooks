About this puzzle

9 x 9 sudoku · Medium level · solvable by logic, no guessing

Fill every row, column and 3 x 3 box with the numbers 1 to 9. Look for the square with the fewest possible numbers, then work outward.

Stuck? Pencil in the possible numbers. If two squares in a row can only be the same two numbers, cross those numbers off the rest of the row. See the medium strategies, with pictures
