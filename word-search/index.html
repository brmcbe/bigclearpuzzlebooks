Questions

Are these word searches really free?

Yes. Each free page has a one-page PDF with the answer key. Print as many as you need for your family or group.

What makes them large print?

Grid letters are 21 point bold and word lists are 18 point, one puzzle per page. Words never run backwards.

Who are they for?

Seniors, adults who like a relaxing puzzle, memory care and assisted living groups, church groups and classrooms.
