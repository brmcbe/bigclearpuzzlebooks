Dementia-friendly printable activities: large-print puzzles

Free large-print word searches and sudoku that are easy to see and easy to start: big letters, nothing backwards, answer keys included. For memory care groups, day programs and caregivers at home.

What makes a puzzle easier to enjoy

  • Big, bold letters. Our grid letters are 21 point bold and word lists are 18 point.
  • One puzzle per page. No crowded pages, plenty of white space.
  • Words never run backwards or up. Only right, down and diagonally down-right.
  • Short word lists. 10 to 12 words per puzzle.
  • Familiar themes. Hymns, Bible stories, Christmas, gardens, kitchens and the 1950s spark conversation.
  • Answer key on page 2. Helpers can give a hint without guessing.

Tips for a group session

  • Print one copy per person, plus one enlarged copy for the table to work on together.
  • Read the word list aloud first and talk about each word: who remembers a sock hop, a soda fountain or a favorite hymn?
  • Pairs work well: one person finds, the other circles.
  • Finishing isn't the goal. Stop while it's still fun.

Questions

What makes a word search easier to do?

Big letters, one puzzle per page, fewer words, familiar themes and words that never run backwards or upward. All of our puzzles follow those rules.

Which free printable should we start with?

Try a theme with strong memories: the 1950s Soda Fountain puzzle, Amazing Grace or Deck the Halls. For number lovers, start with the easy sudoku.

Can we copy them for a group?

Yes, the free printables can be copied for your group. The Activity Director Pack adds 350 puzzles with a facility print license.

Are these puzzles a treatment?

No. They are a relaxing, social activity, not a medical product, and we make no health claims.
